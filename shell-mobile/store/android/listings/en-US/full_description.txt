Omnibox is the Gcalls mobile call center app for businesses. Sales and customer care teams can make and receive calls through the company phone system from anywhere, right on their phone.

Key features:
• Make and receive calls through your company's call center numbers (VoIP), keeping personal numbers private.
• Start a call straight from phone number links (tel:) or Gcalls links.
• Gcalls contacts synced to the phone address book to recognise customers.
• Keeps the screen on and routes audio to speaker or Bluetooth headsets during calls.
• Notifications for calls and important events.

Permissions used:
• Microphone: for voice calls.
• Contacts: to sync Gcalls contacts (optional).
• Notifications: call and event alerts.
Omnibox does not replace your device's default phone app and does not read your call log.

A Gcalls account provided by your company is required. Learn more: https://gcalls.co
