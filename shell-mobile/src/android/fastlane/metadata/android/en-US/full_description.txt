GCalls is a comprehensive software solution designed to digitize business telephone systems, enabling businesses to manage their calls and evaluate the KPIs of their sales and customer service teams. This leads to improved service quality and increased customer satisfaction.

The main purpose of the GCalls app is to provide businesses with a secure and reliable communication platform for outgoing calls. The app may prompt the user to set it as the default phone handler on their device to ensure all outgoing calls are made through the app, allowing businesses to take full advantage of its powerful call management features.

It's important to note that without setting the GCalls app as the default handler, users will not be able to use any of its features.

Here is the list of core features and their benefits:

1. Default phone handling: The app may prompt the user to set it as the default phone handler on their device, allowing all outgoing calls to be made through the app.

2. Productivity and convenience: By using the GCalls app, employees can handle outgoing calls from anywhere through the company telephony system, enhancing their productivity and convenience.

3. User privacy: The app prioritizes user privacy by accessing only the minimum amount of user data necessary to provide its core functionality(just the phone number to process call through the GCalls app). It does not access any other user data.

4. Secure and reliable communication platform: The GCalls app provides a secure and reliable communication platform, making it an essential tool for businesses looking to streamline their outgoing call operations, increase productivity, and improve customer satisfaction.

5. Limited permissions: The app only requires two main permissions - process outgoing calls (set your app as the default handler) and audio (to allow for voice communication through the app).

Use cases for the GCalls app include:

- Sales teams making outbound sales calls
- Customer service teams handling outbound customer calls
- Remote teams making calls from anywhere in the world

By accessing only the minimum amount of user data necessary to provide its core functionality, GCalls prioritizes user privacy. 

Overall, the GCalls app provides a valuable tool for businesses looking to streamline their outgoing call operations, increase productivity, and improve customer satisfaction.
