2. How we use data

  • Provide the Services: connect calls and messages, route them to the right agent, show call history, keep the screen on and manage audio during calls.

  • AI features (if enabled by your organization): transcription, summaries, automated replies or calls by AI agents, quality analysis. AI agents always identify themselves as automated where the law requires.

3. Sharing

We share personal data only with:

  • your organization (the Customer) and its authorized administrators;
  • telecommunications carriers and messaging platforms needed to deliver calls and messages;
  • service providers that host, store, secure or process data for us (for example cloud hosting, speech-to-text and AI model providers) under contracts that require confidentiality and appropriate safeguards;
  • authorities when required by law, or to protect rights, safety and security;
  • a successor in a merger or acquisition, with notice to you.

4. Retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above. Account data is kept while the account is active. Call and message records, recordings and AI-generated data are kept for the period set by your organization (typically up to 12 months) or as required by law, then deleted or anonymized. Backups are overwritten on a rolling basis.

5. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS/SRTP where supported) and protected at rest. Access is limited to authorized staff on a need-to-know basis, logged and reviewed. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure; we will notify you and the competent authority of a personal data breach where the law requires.
