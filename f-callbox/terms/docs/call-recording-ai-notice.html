Effective date: {{effectiveDate}} · {{appName}} by {{brandName}}

Call recording

Organizations using {{appName}} may record calls and keep voicemail for quality, training, compliance and dispute resolution. When recording is enabled:

  • callers are informed at the start of the call (for example by an announcement) where the law requires notice or consent;
  • recordings are encrypted, stored for the retention period set by the organization and accessible only to authorized users;
  • you may ask the organization you spoke with, or us at {{privacyEmail}}, for access to or deletion of a recording, subject to legal retention duties.

AI agents and automated processing

Some calls and messages may be answered, made or assisted by AI agents, and calls may be transcribed or summarized automatically. In that case:

  • the AI agent identifies itself as automated at the start of the interaction where the law requires;
  • you can ask to speak to a human at any time, and the agent will transfer you or arrange a call-back where available;
  • AI outputs (transcripts, summaries, answers) may contain errors and are reviewed by people for important decisions;
  • your communication data is not used to train third-party general-purpose AI models without the organization's agreement and, where required, your consent;
  • decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on you are not made solely by automated means.

Your choices

You may object to recording or automated processing by ending the call, asking for a human, or contacting {{privacyEmail}}. See the Privacy Policy for your full rights.
