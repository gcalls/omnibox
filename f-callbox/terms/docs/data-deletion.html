Account and Data Deletion

Effective date: {{effectiveDate}} · {{appName}} by {{legalName}}

You can ask us to delete your {{appName}} account and associated personal data at any time. This page explains how, what is deleted and what we may keep.

How to request deletion

  1. Email {{privacyEmail}} from the email address of your account with the subject "Delete my {{appName}} account", and include your tenant (organization) name.
  2. Or ask your organization's {{brandName}} administrator to remove your user; this deletes your account the same way.
  3. We confirm your identity and complete the request within 30 days, and confirm by email.

Uninstalling the app does not delete your account. You can also ask to delete only certain data (for example call recordings or AI transcripts) without deleting the account.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, email, phone number, user ID and sign-in credentials.
  • Device data linked to you: push tokens and app diagnostics.
  • Call recordings, transcripts and AI summaries in which you are the account user, unless your organization must retain them.

What may be kept, and for how long

  • Call and message records (time, number, duration) and invoices needed for billing, tax and telecommunications obligations — up to the period required by law, then deleted.
  • Data your organization is legally required to keep, held under its control.
  • Backups, which are overwritten within 90 days.
  • Anonymized statistics that no longer identify you.
